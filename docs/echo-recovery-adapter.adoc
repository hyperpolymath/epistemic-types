= Epistemic recovery and canonical Echo recovery
:revdate: 2026-11-07

== Contract and dependency boundary

`tests/integration/CanonicalRecovery.agda` imports the actual owning
`EpistemicTypes.EchoBridge.Recovery` and `EchoFiniteResidue.ExactRecovery`
records. For a fixed `Retention A B R`, both require a total curried decoder
and the same source-value round trip. The adapter specializes Echo's
universe-polymorphic contract to Epistemic's `Set` carriers. It does not
generalize the Epistemic core to arbitrary universe levels.

The two adapters copy both fields. `fromToRecovery` and
`toFromExactRecovery` prove full record inverses by record eta, including
the supplied round-trip proofs. `decoderAgrees` covers every visible/residue
input, including unreachable inputs. `roundtripProofAgrees` preserves the
particular proof supplied by the caller. Neither proof uses function
extensionality, proof irrelevance, choice, or a constructed extension from a
partial decoder.

The retention contract, its certificate family, and its soundness witness
stay fixed. Recovery does not consume a certificate, infer its intended
meaning, or follow from certificate validity. The existing
`CanonicalEcho` representation conversion separately preserves the residue
and certificate. No equality witness in the dependent fibre is reconstructed.

The bridge lives in the optional integration tree so the 23-module core
remains builtins-only. Echo does not acquire an Epistemic dependency.
This is an identification of two existing interfaces, not a new recovery
principle or a claim of novelty.

== Transferred theorem and controls

[cols="2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Declaration |Exact scope
|`recoveryFiniteFibreBound`
|An injected family of `n` sources in one observation fibre, residues in
`Fin k`, and a supplied recovery imply `n ≤ k`. The family need not enumerate
the fibre. This reuses Echo's finite pigeonhole theorem; cost is alphabet
cardinality, not bits, entropy, time, or physical information loss.
|`recoveryForbidsResidueCollision`
|Two distinct sources with the same observation cannot have the same residue
under a supplied exact recovery. This is a necessary condition; no general
construction of recovery from collision-freedom is asserted.
|`twoSymbolBound`, `twoCertificatePreserved`
|Two sources in one visible fibre retain their own `Fin 2` values. The
certificate restricts observations to that fibre, recovery attains `2 ≤ 2`,
and canonical residue conversion preserves the original certificate proof.
|`noOneSymbolRecovery`
|The constant observation and constant one-symbol residue on `Fin 2`
cannot support recovery, despite every generated residue being certified.
|`emptyCertifiedDecoder`, `noEmptyRecovery`, `noEmptyExactRecovery`
|With empty source, singleton observation/residue, and empty certificates,
certificate-restricted decoding and its round trip are vacuous. Total
decoding is impossible. A certified-only or reachable-only replacement would
therefore change the contract.
|`decodersAgreeOnImage`, `decodersDisagreeOffImage`, `adapterPreservesOffImage`
|Two Bool decoders satisfy the same round-trip law and agree on every
generated pair, yet differ at an unreachable pair. The adapter preserves
that difference; on-image agreement alone does not identify total decoders.
|===

These statements concern source values and mathematical functions.
Execution provenance, authorization, secret visibility, runtime ownership,
linear consumption and independent proof-assistant translations remain
separate obligations. Ordinal research is outside this work.
