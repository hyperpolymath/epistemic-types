== Findings

. **Issue #32 is not obsolete, and it was not transferred.** This repository's
  note said the issue "is obsolete as titled" and that "the replacement issue is
  the transferred #32 itself". No transfer occurred (issue write access was
  documented as denied with `403` in the migration commit message), and no
  ruling makes the issue obsolete: the placement decision moves the *work*, not
  the issue. The note now says #32 stays here and stays open. Corrected in
  `docs/secret-types.adoc`.
. **`STATE.a2ml` described a pending transfer.** It read "the transfer of
  epistemic-types#32 to secret-types is pending GitHub issue-write access and
  must not be duplicated". There is no transfer to wait for; corrected to
  "issue #32 stays in this repository and stays open (no transfer)".
. **`META.a2ml` ADR-007 and `0-AI-MANIFEST.a2ml` used "move" language** ("the
  move of issue #32 must not be duplicated"). The non-duplication intent is
  kept; the transfer implication is removed.
. **The four "ready-to-post" coordination texts named in `secret-types` PR #4
  were never persisted to any repository** (`secret-types#2`,
  `nextgen-typing#118`, `valence-shell#210`, and the transferred-issue
  provenance note). Only this review's proposed update for `epistemic-types#32`
  is prepared in text. Recorded here as a process gap, not as a claim about the
  other repositories.
. **Downstream trackers are stale; ownership stays with them.**
  `nextgen-typing` `docs/TYPE-CONNECTIONS.adoc` (245 lines, checked 2026-10-05)
  carries no secret-types row, although D153 and `nextgen-typing#118` both
  require family-map registration. `valence-shell` `docs/THEORY-FEED.adoc`
  (merged via PR #211) still describes `secret-types` as un-minted, records
  "mint it … or fold it into epistemic-types#32" as an open owner call, and
  grades its feed `?`/`?`; `valence-shell#92` remains unselected as a consumer.
  Anytype#15 remains open for the grade-instance neighbour comparison; SystemET
  PR #17 is still merged at `a11c98774c8c4a1b35c1e3bd9145a191dffb3840`
  (2026-07-21). These repositories own their own corrections.
. **The statements about this repository's interfaces stay here.** The
  standalone specification explicitly says the origin note's statements about
  `Access.Preorder`, `BeliefModality`, `Warrant`, `EchoBridge.Grade` "remain
  statements about *that* repository's narrower interfaces. They are not
  re-homed here, and none of them is a confidentiality guarantee." Nothing in
  the transition weakens them.
