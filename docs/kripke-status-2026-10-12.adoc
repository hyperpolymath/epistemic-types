= Kripke semantics and warrant soundness — status 2026-10-12

README lists *complete epistemic logic / Kripke semantics* and *global
soundness of warrants* as intentionally unformalised. The three modules
under `src/EpistemicTypes/Kripke/` change that status as follows.

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| ID | Claim | Status
| E-1 | `Modality` cannot express Kripke knowledge: its carrier has no world index. `IndexedModality`/`IndexedFactive`/`IndexedAccessible` are `Base`/`Access` field for field with `E : K → (W → Set) → (W → Set)`; the originals are the `W = ⊤` case (`toModality`) | PROVEN (`Kripke/Indexed.agda`)
| E-2 | Frames and `Box`; `Box` is an `IndexedModality` | PROVEN (`Kripke/Frame.agda`)
| E-3 | Correspondences, constructively: reflexive ⇔ factive (T, both directions); transitive ⇔ positively introspective (4, both directions); Euclidean ⇒ 5; serial ⇒ D. The converses of 5 and D need classical reasoning and are not claimed | PROVEN / OPEN as stated
| E-4 | A reflexive frame yields an `IndexedFactive` (`factive`) | PROVEN
| E-5 | *The non-factive model*: two worlds, access only w₀ → w₁; at w₀ there is a `Warrant` (evidence: a `Box` proof) for a false proposition, and no `sound` field can exist for it. `Warrant ≠ SoundWarrant`, witnessed | PROVEN (`Kripke/Belief.agda`: `believed`, `false-at-w₀`, `no-sound-warrant`); TESTED (`tests/reject/BeliefIsKnowledge`: factivity refused on this frame)
| E-6 | Global soundness of warrants in a reflexive frame — every type-determined warrant sound — is discharged externally in choreographic-types (`Choreographic.Kripke`, which imports this repo's `Warrant` at 495db9c) | PROVEN there; recorded here
|===

Separation (family bar, criterion 2): the belief model is a modality with
evidence and no extraction, which no grade algebra and no secret label can
express; the factive/non-factive split is the family's own.

Builtins only; checked with the repo's own flags (`--safe --without-K
--double-check -W error`).
